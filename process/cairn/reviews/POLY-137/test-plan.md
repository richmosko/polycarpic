## Notes for the builder

- The new DB suites use a duck-typed mailer (`tests/db/helpers/mail.ts`), so they need neither `@poly/mail` nor its fake to exist; `packages/mail/src/mail.test.ts` and the worker tests do.
- Five tests are green on arrival and say why in their title or here: the `/magic-link/verify` limiter (the magic-link plugin already ships a 5-a-minute rule; the new `customRules` key is pinned in `options-137.test.ts`), `RESET_PASSWORD_DISABLED` with no mailer, a failing mailer not changing the magic-link answer, `accountLockout` not disabled, the raw-pathname pair, the lockfile and pins. Each is a guard against regression; `red-run.md` shows the mutation for the ones that would otherwise be unproved.
- To add a TOTP to a user whose first method is a passkey, `/two-factor/verify-totp` answers 500 `email_confirmation_required` today (an existing behaviour, not under test). `auth-method-hold.test.ts` flips the two columns in one transaction as `poly_auth` (`set_two_factor_enabled` first, then `verified`), which is the same pair of writes.
- `issue_email_challenge` returns NULL after 3 challenges in 15 minutes, so the 11th-send-code test ages earlier challenges by SQL; the cap under test is the mailer's.
