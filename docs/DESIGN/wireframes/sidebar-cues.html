A — chevron at rail bottom — CHOSEN
›

Always-visible chevron, click to toggle. Most discoverable; small permanent cost. Card now stretches to fill the viewport height under the header (shown here), chevron sits at the very bottom of that full-height card, not just below the nav items.

B — hover-reveal — rejected
← flies out on hover, pin to persist

Zero permanent chrome; needs a tap fallback on touch devices (no hover).

C — keyboard + remembered state — rejected

Cmd/Ctrl+B toggles, state persists (localStorage, same pattern as toc.js's theme toggle). One-time tooltip teaches it. No permanent control at all — least discoverable without the tooltip.
